I developed "Predict-to-Prevent," an interactive causal inference web application designed to shift Bangladesh's diabetes management from reactive treatment to evidence-based prevention.It translates causal machine learning research into a practical diabetes risk reduction tool. Built with Python and Streamlit, the platform utilizes Double Machine Learning (DML) and Causal Inference—grounded in BDHS data to simulate how lifestyle interventions directly lower health risks. The dashboard specifically demonstrates the power of weight loss, showing that even minor BMI reductions can significantly decrease an individual’s diabetes risk trajectory while saving annual out-of-pocket medical costs. On a national scale, the model quantifies that if 10 million people reduce their BMI by just 1 unit, it could prevent 94,000 new cases and save the Bangladesh economy over 239 Crore BDT annually. By bridging the gap between data science and public policy, this tool provides the actionable intelligence needed to combat the rising diabetes burden.
